We teach obedience to the will of Christ as the way of salvation — taught from Scripture rather than from ritual. It follows a clear path.

Faith

Faith in Christ, which comes by hearing the gospel.

Plan your visit

What to expect when you visit with us.

Many people feel hesitant about visiting an unfamiliar church. So that you can come with ease, here is exactly what to expect.

Courtesy and kindness

Apart from reserved seats for the leadership and choir, no seats are set aside for anyone. Every visitor and member is welcomed with courtesy and kindness.

You will not find entertainment or religious showmanship. Our time together is spent studying the Word, in prayer, and in singing — with words that are easy to understand and sung sincerely from the heart.

Acts 10:34–35Galatians 3:28Ephesians 5:191 Corinthians 14:15Colossians 3:16

Order and reverence

The order of service is clear and unhurried; good order marks every gathering. On the Lord's Day you will see us share bread and the fruit of the vine in memory of Christ's death — we do this on the first Sunday of each month, proclaiming the Lord's death until He comes.

Offerings are simple: two freewill collections, one before the message and one after. We are supported by the freewill offerings and tithes of our members. We do not sell anointing oil, water or other items — our focus is Christ, and seeing the Word impact lives.

1 Corinthians 14:33Matthew 26:26–291 Corinthians 11:26

The Bible — our only guide

The Bible is the text we return to in every sermon and class. You will never be pressured to accept a preacher's opinion — we want you to read for yourself to see “whether those things are so.”

No one is singled out, embarrassed, or asked to testify or give against their will. You are free to quietly observe, study, and grow with us in Christ.

2 Timothy 3:16–17Romans 1:16Acts 17:111 Corinthians 14:40

Bring your Bible

You are more than welcome at every worship and prayer service. Please come and bring your Bible to search the Scriptures with us. If you do not have one, we will gladly help you get a good copy.
